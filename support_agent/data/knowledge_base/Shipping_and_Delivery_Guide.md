# Shipping and Delivery Operations Guide

## Section 1: Standard and Express Delivery
- **Standard Delivery:** Dispatched within 24 hours. Transit time is 3 to 5 business days across domestic zones. Free on orders above $50 or ₹999.
- **Express Courier:** Dispatched same-day for orders placed before 1:00 PM. Transit time is 1 to 2 business days.

## Section 2: Order Tracking
All orders receive a carrier tracking identifier (e.g., TRK-IN-XXXXXX or TRK-US-XXXXXX).
- Real-time milestone updates: Order Placed -> Packed -> Dispatched -> In Transit -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered.
- Customers can inspect tracking in their account or ask the SupportAgent AI with their order ID.

## Section 3: Damaged or Missing Shipments
If an order arrives with physical box damage or missing items:
- Customers must report within 48 hours of delivery.
- Photos of external packaging and shipping label must be uploaded.
- Priority replacement or instant refund is dispatched upon inspection.
