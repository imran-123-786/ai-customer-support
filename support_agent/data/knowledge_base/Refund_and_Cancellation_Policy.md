# SupportAgent Global Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Section 1: Overview and Scope
SupportAgent adheres to a customer-first policy ensuring fair, transparent, and prompt resolution of payment disputes, product returns, and cancellations.

## Section 2: 30-Day Return Window
Customers may return physical products within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery for a full refund or exchange.
- The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories.
- Standard return shipping is complimentary for defective or damaged items.
- Returns requested after 30 calendar days cannot be processed through automated self-service and require managerial review.

## Section 3: Duplicate Charges and Billing Errors
If a customer is charged twice for the same order due to a gateway timeout or network retry:
- The system automatically flags duplicate transactions.
- Customer support will immediately verify payment gateway transaction IDs.
- The duplicate charge will be refunded immediately without requiring physical return.
- Processing timeframe: 3 to 5 business days back to the original payment source (UPI, Credit Card, or Net Banking).

## Section 4: Order Cancellations
- Orders in **Pending** or **Processing** status can be cancelled immediately with a 100% full refund before shipment dispatch.
- Orders in **Shipped** or **In Transit** status cannot be intercepted in transit; the customer must receive the parcel and initiate a standard return.

## Section 5: Non-Refundable Items
The following items are strictly non-refundable unless defective upon arrival:
- Digital gift cards and software activation licenses.
- Personalized or custom-engraved merchandise.
